    How the ranking works, and what this prototype leaves out

    Score. Each sign gets a weight: signs listed under many conditions (dizziness, nausea) count less than signs listed under few (hot dry skin, rigid abdomen). For each condition the page computes two things:

    • Explains: the weighted share of your entries that the condition lists.
    • Matches: the weighted share of the condition's listed signs that you entered.

    The match score is the harmonic mean of the two. It is not a probability. When scores tie, conditions the manual says to evacuate sooner rank higher.

    Situation chips (dashed outline) are not signs listed in the manual. They come from each chapter's scope, such as "hot environment" for the heat illness chapter. They carry the same weight as signs.

    Evacuation tags repeat the manual's own wording: "Evacuate immediately", "Evacuate (as soon as possible)", "Seek medical aid", or no evacuation listed.

    Not included

    • Ch 16–22 (head and abdominal injuries, impalement, snakebite, anaphylaxis treatment) could not be retrieved for this test.
    • Ch 9 (nerve agents) and Ch 24–27 (eye injuries) were left out as outside the test's scope.
    • Asthma: TC 4-02.1 has no asthma chapter.
    • Ch 10 and Ch 13 entries are close summaries of the manual's text. Other chapters use short verbatim excerpts.
